# Course description (for marketing)

## Title

**From Requirements to Running Software: BA-Driven Development with AI**

_Subtitle:_ How AI-enabled business analysts will drive the development process

## Description

AI can now turn a natural-language request into working software in hours. Type "build me an app that sorts my
email" and you'll get something that runs. But does it do what the business actually needs? The oldest rule in
computing still holds: garbage in, garbage out. A loose request leaves the AI to guess: unstated business rules,
missed exceptions, and decisions no stakeholder ever made.

Give the same AI the work of a skilled business analyst instead, with requirements built on proven BA standards and
techniques, and the result changes. The software reflects real stakeholder needs, and every rule can be traced and
tested. Capturing those needs precisely and completely is exactly what BAs do best.

That puts the BA at the center of AI-enabled development: no longer just the person who feeds in information and
checks the result, but the person who drives the product.

In this hands-on workshop, you'll experience that shift for yourself. Working through a realistic case study,
EmailManager (an app that triages a busy inbox using business rules), you'll use structured BA artifacts to direct
an AI assistant as it builds a running application on your own laptop:

- use cases
- user stories with acceptance criteria
- business rules
- decision tables

You won't write code or need a developer on standby. You make the business decisions, review each milestone, and
watch your requirements become working software, complete with automated tests that prove every business rule.

You'll leave with your own working app, a reusable kit for your next project, and a clear view of the BA's
expanding role in an AI-driven world.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. **Describe the emerging role of the AI-enabled BA:** how it shifts from information provider and verifier to the
   driver of product development, and why requirements quality matters more than ever.
2. **Write requirements an AI can build from reliably**, using use cases, user stories with Given/When/Then
   acceptance criteria, business rules and decision tables, and spot the gaps that force the AI to guess.
3. **Direct an AI assistant through a milestone-based build:** answer its clarifying questions, approve its plan, and
   review a working result at each checkpoint, with no coding and minimal technical knowledge.
4. **Verify that the software does what the business needs**, by tracing decision tables and acceptance criteria to
   automated tests, and decide where human judgment, approvals and data privacy must stay in the loop.

## Course outline

**Session 1: from requirements to a running app** (3.5 hours)

| #   | Module                                       | What you'll do                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **The BA in an AI-enabled world** (20 min)   | Garbage in, garbage out: the same app built from a one-line request vs. from structured BA requirements. From requirements inputter to product driver: what changes for BAs, and what doesn't |
| 2   | **Requirements AI can build from** (25 min)  | Tour the EmailManager case study: use cases, user stories, business rules and decision tables. The common gaps that lead AI astray                                                            |
| 3   | **Kick off your build** (20 min)             | Start your own project with a single sentence, and answer the AI's clarifying questions as the BA                                                                                             |
| 4   | **Driving the build** (60 min, with a break) | Approve the milestone plan, follow along as the AI works, and see how each requirement shapes what gets built                                                                                 |
| 5   | **Your running app** (30 min)                | Explore your working app on a demo mailbox, and trace screens and behavior back to your requirements                                                                                          |
| 6   | **Proving it works** (30 min)                | Turn decision tables and acceptance criteria into automated tests that verify every business rule (a preview of session 2)                                                                    |
| 7   | **The road ahead** (15 min)                  | Governance, approvals and privacy when AI builds software. Taking the approach back to your organization                                                                                      |

**Session 2 (optional): business rules in action** (3.5 hours)

- Build the rules engine from your decision tables, with an automated test for every rule.
- Review the test coverage as a BA: is every rule and scenario covered?
- Add AI-assisted categorization and see your rules applied to an inbox.
- Optional homework: connect a real (test) Gmail account.

## Who should attend

Business analysts, product owners, business architects and project managers who want to lead AI-enabled delivery. No
programming experience is required.

**What you need:** a Mac on which you can install apps (a personal Mac is recommended). Access to the AI assistant is
provided. Setup instructions are sent before the workshop.
